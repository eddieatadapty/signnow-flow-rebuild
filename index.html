Day 1

Pass the campaign and keyword into Adapty

After a one-time SDK setup, the YouTube campaign or the Apple Ads keyword each install came from arrives as a user attribute and picks the flow at first open. No app release.

Week 1

One flow per promise

Sender, signer and two-party agreement each get their own first screens; your brand search “signnow” keeps today’s sign-up first for people who already have an account.

Week 2

Judge each source on what it earns

Each flow against today’s, measured on trials, paid subscriptions and what each install from that ad or search earns, so spend follows the promise that pays.

All three phones run the whole flow with the five ideas. On Apple Ads you hold about 97% of US searches for your own name, a strong brand defence. Some terms with a high share are words from legal documents, not people looking for a signing app: “terms_of_use” and “termsofuse” (80%), “section” (64%), “eula” (21%), “access”, “issuer”. Worth checking what those installs earn. The search data is account-level, so it can’t tell SignNow from SignNow Private Cloud. This layer carries no range: only a test per ad can give one.
